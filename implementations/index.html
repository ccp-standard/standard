Implementations

Platforms that implement
this standard.

The standard describes an architectural model. An implementation operationalizes it. The two are not the same, and a listing here is a claim about capabilities — not an endorsement of a vendor.

How conformance is claimed

Evaluated against the minimum capabilities.

The following platforms have been evaluated against the six minimum capability requirements defined by this standard. Listings are maintained by the CCP Initiative.

What a listing does not assert Conformance describes the capabilities a platform provides. It does not assert that any particular deployment governs the whole of an organization's cryptographic landscape — coverage grows as applications are onboarded, and is a question for the maturity model.

Nor does a listing imply a one-to-one relationship between platform capabilities and CAPA pillars. A single capability may contribute to several pillars, and each pillar may depend on several capabilities working together.
Reference implementation

ANKASecure©

A Crypto Agility Orchestration Platform developed by ANKATech Solutions INC. It operationalizes the Cryptographic Control Plane architecture: governed applications invoke cryptographic operations using key identifiers, while the platform resolves the applicable policy, lifecycle state and cryptographic mechanism, then coordinates execution through the appropriate cryptographic infrastructure.

Its operational capabilities contribute across the CAPA pillars rather than mapping to them individually. Cryptographic execution, key custody and Roots of Trust may remain distributed across designated HSMs, KMS platforms and trust domains, including PKCS#11-based environments, with the platform providing a common governance and orchestration layer above them. It supports a broad, extensible portfolio of cryptographic mechanisms spanning classical, hybrid and post-quantum cryptography, including NIST FIPS 203/204/205 (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) with hybrid coexistence.

Community

Submit an implementation

If your platform provides the minimum capability requirements defined by this standard, we invite you to submit it for review and listing. The CCP Initiative evaluates conformance claims against the six requirements defined on the Control Plane page.

Note in particular requirement 05: a platform that requires all keys or Roots of Trust to reside in a single provider, repository or cryptographic failure domain does not satisfy this standard.
